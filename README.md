# Gary George

**Product manager who ships.** I build small AI systems that turn information overload into something you can navigate.

The same problem keeps showing up in different costumes — too many newsletters, too many podcasts, too many talks to remember which one said the thing. So I build tools that read the pile and hand back the shape of it.

Most of my work lives at the interaction of three primitives:

- **LLMs** — extraction
- **Synthesis** — turning raw pulls into something with a point of view
- **Knowledge graphs, clustering, and embeddings** — woven together as structure and memory

Increasingly it runs on data: **LLM-as-judge** evaluation, visualization, and the analytics instincts I built over years of business intelligence work. I think PMs who can prototype with AI build better products, so I ship code — and the thinking lives in the README, not just the demo.
