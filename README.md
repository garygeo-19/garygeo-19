# Gary George

**Maker · builder · artist.**

I love to see ideas come to life. My passions include data, visualizations and micro-learning, but sometimes I just follow my curiosity. Lately that means micro-learning apps built end to end: product, data, code, and the art. I'd rather ship something small and learn from it than write a long spec.

I also consult through **[Recurxive LLC](https://recurxive.com)**, building AI integrations and data solutions for teams. That work draws on 25+ years in product and data strategy, including roles at Intuit and Indeed, on analytics products, data platforms, taxonomies, and knowledge graphs.

`TypeScript` `Python` `React Native / Expo` `p5.js` `Three.js / WebGL` `LLM APIs` `MCP` `knowledge graphs` `data viz`

## Ship log

- `next` **FlashFeed lesson videos**: short vertical videos generated from FlashFeed lessons. Each frame is a function of time keyed to the narration, so the visuals re-time themselves when the voice changes.<br>`canvas` `headless Chromium` `ffmpeg`
- `2026‑09` **[FractalSprites](https://github.com/garygeo-19/FractalSprites)**: turns transparent sprites into kaleidoscopes, mirror tunnels, and Mandelbrot and Julia fractals that sample the sprites' own colors, with mesh morphs between shapes. It has six themed worlds and exports 720p or 1080p clips with a soundtrack.<br>`p5.js` `WebGL` `Vite` `MediaRecorder`
- `2026‑09` **Projection mapping experiments**: generative p5.js visuals mapped onto the faces of a cube, with per-face corner calibration that can be saved and reloaded. It's a first step toward projecting onto physical objects.<br>`p5.js` `p5.mapper`
- `2026‑06` **[The Future of Learning](https://pub.towardsai.net/the-future-of-learning-0e46f6497a79)**: an essay on how AI changes learning in three steps: sourcing knowledge, shaping it for a specific learner, and delivering it through media, interfaces, and conversation.<br>`essay` `Towards AI`
- `2026‑06` **[KaleidoFlow](https://github.com/garygeo-19/KaleidoFlow)**: analyzes a whole track offline (tempo, beats, loudness bands), then pushes about 262k GPU particles through a curl-noise flow field folded into kaleidoscope symmetry. The music drives light and color rather than motion, so the image flows instead of jittering.<br>`React` `Three.js` `GPGPU` `Web Audio`
- `2026‑06` **[Image Source-cery](https://github.com/garygeo-19/image-source-cery)**: works through a ranked list of image providers (Wikimedia, iNaturalist, generation, and others) and has a vision-LLM judge approve each candidate. The first one that passes wins, and license and attribution travel with every image.<br>`TypeScript` `CLI` `vision LLM`
- `2026‑06` **[Emoji2ASCII](https://github.com/garygeo-19/Emoji2ASCII)**: rasterizes text, emoji, or a photo and repaints it with random characters in the source's colors. It exports images, plain text, animations, or a link that reproduces your settings.<br>`vanilla JS` `single HTML file` `zero deps`
- `2026‑01` **[AbsurdScene: Spanish](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/absurdscene-spanish/id6757897065)** released on the App Store: 198 lessons across 33 scenes at three levels, with matching warm-ups, visual multiple-choice questions, and streaks.<br>`iOS` `Python`
- `2025‑12` **[Cross-Threaded Newsletter Navigator](https://github.com/garygeo-19/cross-threaded-newsletter-navigator)**: pulls newsletters from Gmail, splits them into individual stories with Gemini, then embeds and clusters them to show the week's news as an interactive treemap. I built it for a Google AI course.<br>`Python` `Flask` `Gemini` `sentence-transformers` `DBSCAN`
- `2025` **[Family Tree Art Studio](https://www.instagram.com/familytreeartstudio/)**: personalized family-tree art. Each family is modeled as a knowledge graph, laid out as a tree, and rendered with AI in styles from charcoal sketch to folk art.<br>`knowledge graphs` `p5.js` `SVG` `generative AI`
- `2025‑04` **[10 Ways LLMs Can Fail Your Organization](https://pub.towardsai.net/10-ways-llms-can-fail-your-organization-bc855d2061b5)**: what goes wrong when an organization puts a chatbot in front of its users, and what to watch for.<br>`essay` `Towards AI`
- `2024‑08` **[When AI Artists Compete](https://medium.com/data-science/when-ai-artists-compete-e5898a507718)**: AI agent "artists" write p5.js generative art, and an AI judge picks winners over rounds of competition.<br>`essay` `Towards Data Science` `p5.js`
- `2024‑07` **[AI Trends on TED](https://pub.towardsai.net/ai-trends-on-ted-0033ea18173c)**: an analysis of 550+ AI-related TED talks going back to 2007, with the dataset and an interactive [knowledge-graph app](https://github.com/garygeo-19/AI-TED-Talks-KG).<br>`essay` `Towards AI` `JavaScript`
- `2024‑01` **[Decoding the Bestsellers](https://medium.com/@garygeo/decoding-the-bestsellers-a-comparative-analysis-of-top-self-help-books-across-major-platforms-fb75b2a4b536)**: compares top self-help book lists from Audible and other platforms to see where they agree.<br>`essay` `Medium`
